Rwanda Quick Facts

  • 🇷🇼 Known as "Land of a Thousand Hills" — breathtaking landscapes at every turn.
  • 🦍 Home to the endangered mountain gorillas in Volcanoes National Park.
  • 💧 Lake Kivu is one of Africa's deepest lakes, offering serene beauty and adventure.
  • 🎨 The Intore dance is a traditional Rwandan performance symbolizing bravery.
  • ☕ Rwanda’s high-altitude coffee is considered some of the finest in the world.
